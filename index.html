Privacy Policy — Study Progress

Effective date: September 29, 2026

Study Progress is developed by Emerson.

Information We Collect

Study Progress does not require you to create an account and does not collect personal information such as your name, email address, phone number, or location.

The app allows you to enter study information such as subject names, total pages, and pages completed. This information is stored locally on your device so that your study progress remains available when you reopen the app.

How We Use Information

The study information entered into Study Progress is used only to provide the app's study-progress tracking functionality.

Data Sharing

Study Progress does not transmit or share your study information with the developer or third parties.

Internet and Third-Party Services

Study Progress does not require an internet connection and does not use advertising, analytics, or third-party data-collection services.

Data Retention and Deletion

Your study information remains stored locally on your device until you delete it through the app or remove the app's data or uninstall the app.

Children's Privacy

Study Progress does not knowingly collect personal information from children or adults.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

This privacy policy may be updated when the app's functionality or data practices change. Any updated policy will be published at the same public URL.
